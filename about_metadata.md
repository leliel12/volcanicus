The static Holocene list only carries 3 of those (no VEI/style history), so I implemented an equal-weight 3-criteria approximation (tectonic setting, dominant rock type, morphology; 1/3 each), scored against the entire 1439-volcano GVP list (not just your 198), tie-broken by geographic distance, top 5 kept.
